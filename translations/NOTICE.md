# Qt 6 Italian translation catalogue

The compiled qtbase_it.qm catalogue comes from the official MSYS2 package mingw-w64-x86_64-qt6-translations 6.11.2-1, retrieved on 20 September 2026. The package SHA-256 is recorded in install-qt6.ps1 and was verified before extraction.

Package and source links: https://packages.msys2.org/packages/mingw-w64-x86_64-qt6-translations and https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qttranslations.git/ .

QTranslator loads this Qt 6 catalogue directly. Application-specific Italian/English strings remain in the C++ sources. The prior Qt 5 TS parser and catalogue are not used in this edition.
